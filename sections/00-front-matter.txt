If something is blue, it might need more love.
If a word is gold that means it has many branches but is not its own root
word. They are not counted in the word count.
Words in pink are loan words used by natives. They are not counted in the
word count. (LW)
If something is green then it is the name of a city or a question word.
(City names are not counted in the word count)
Masculine nouns with an "(a)" marker will keep their "A" in the Genitive
Plural Form i.e. "Metra" (CF) -> "Metara" (Gen Pl) (Like an "(+a)"
marker.) (Some Masculine nouns have the "(+a)" marker by mistake.)
A verb with "se" is bulleted underneath the unreflexive version, if there
is no version that is non-reflexive, it is its own word, this is to reduce
redundancy and also show the relationship between the reflexive and
non-reflexive versions.
Words that have a minimized extension like "brod -> brodić" have the
minimized extension bulleted underneath with the minimized extension not
in the word count, however words that do have a separate meaning are not
bulleted underneath and are counted to the word count. (Not every word
will have a minimization bulleted underneath, even if it might have one.
Just words that make sense to have a bullet.) (Words in this category will
not have the "(e)" marker because it is assumed.)
Words ending in c,č,ć,dž,đ,j,š,ž most likely have (e), words ending in
anything else are assumed to have (o).
