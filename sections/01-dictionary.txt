Key to Dictionary Markings
Noun (Noun)
●  Irregular (Ir) (Words with
this marker have special
attributes)
●  Bosnian (Bos) (If a word has
this marker it is used
primarily in Bosnia and
Herzegovina.)
●  Croatian (Hrv) (If a word has
this maker it is used
primarily in Croatia.)
●  Montenegrin (Crn) (If a word
has this marker it is used
primarily in Montenegro. If a
word does not have a
Montenegrin Marker, the
Serbian one represents it.)
●  Serbian (Srp) (If a word has
this marker it is used
primarily in Serbia.)
●  Male (M) (Words ending with a
consonant are male unless
otherwise stated, if a word
has the male marker it is
only there for clarification
where there might be
confusion.)
●  Female (F) (Words ending with
an "a" are female unless
otherwise stated, if a word
has the feminine marker, this
means a similar word is a
different gender and it is
only there for
clarification.)
●  Neuter (Neu) (Words ending in
an "e" or "o" are neuter
unless otherwise stated, if a
word has the neuter marker,
this means a similar word is
a different gender and it is
only there for clarification,
for example "veće" and
"većer".)
●  Feminine Extra (FE) (Words
with this marker are Feminine
and take special case
endings.)(Fem 4th)
●  Male Extra (ME) (Words with
this marker are Feminine with
Masculine Adjectives.) (Masc
5th)
●  Male - Animate, Inanimate
(Anim) (Inan) (If a word
lacks this marker, Inanimate
is assumed.)
●  Male - ov/ev ending for
plural (+ov) (+ev) (If a word
has the (+ov) or (+ev)
marker, the respective (o) or
(e) marker is assumed but not
written.)
●  Male - em ending for
Instrumental (o),(e) (If
there is no marker (o) is
assumed.)
●  Disappearing "A" (a)/(No "a")
(Words with the marker (a)
have a Disappearing "A" and
words with (No "a") do not
have a Disappearing "A".)
●  Appearing "A" (+a)
●  "I" ending for Genitive
Plural (i)
●  Loss of "(x)" (-(x)) Words
with this marker lose the
specified "(x)" letter
whether it be a "T", "N" or
something else.
